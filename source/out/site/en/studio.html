Inside the studio

Two workshops, one standard

Design and fabrication sit in separate workshops. The same ruleset, the same quality plan and the same ledger run across both, so a small pilot order and a bulk run follow one process.

People you will actually meet

A design lead, a workshop craftsperson and an export coordinator own each order. The artisan review queue is shared, so a difficult detail gets a named reviewer.

Visit, or walk through on video

Studio visits run on weekdays. If travel is not practical, the export desk can walk you through the workshop, the inspection station and the finishing bench on a call.
