# Competition Operations Pack: Optional Global Design Co-Creation
Status: optional proposal. This document is not part of the contracted M1 to M4
delivery and adds no executable surface to the product.

Requirement: `docs/requirements.json` entry D1, "Optional global design
co-creation competition operations package", milestone M4, optional bonus, scope
note "Scoped after M4", verify group A. Because D1 is optional it carries no
`contract_refs` and no `artifact_refs`. There is no competition command, route, data
file or report in the repository, and this document does not invent one. The pack is
an operating plan the company can adopt after M4 without changing the product
contract. All names below are roles or synthetic examples; no brand, sponsor or
partner is named.
## Purpose and Scope
Run one global design co-creation competition on the IPB-0001 universe so external
designers produce derivative concepts the studio can license, prototype and sell.
The competition is a sourcing channel, not a marketing stunt: every winning entry
must be producible under an existing merchandise specification or a new
specification written before the award. Success means a shortlist that passes
heritage and craft review, at least one design that converts into a signed
derivative licence, and a repeatable operations file for a future edition.

In scope: concept, categories, eligibility, timeline, submission pack, judging, IP
and licensing safeguards, data handling, communications, staffing, budget lines and
a post-event review. Out of scope: changes to the website, CRM, IP records or
licensing records; payment processing, ticketing or a public voting platform; named
sponsors, celebrity judges or media partners; legal advice. Counsel reviews the
terms before launch. The pack runs on the existing product as-is: the site supplies
the public story, the licensing templates supply the contract skeleton, and the
character bible supplies the design constraints.

more than two weeks, the company announces the new date once, in writing, to every
registered entrant.
## Eligibility and Submission
Eligibility: entrant is 18 or older and can sign a contract in their own name; the
entry is original and does not reproduce a protected ritual object, a sacred symbol
or a third-party character; the entrant has cleared rights for every asset; employees
of the company and their immediate families are excluded; a team entry names one
lead entrant who receives communications and prizes.

Submission pack: a three page PDF design board at A3 landscape and 150 dpi; a one page
vector motif sheet showing the motif and its construction; a material statement covering
materials, finishes, durability and recyclability; a 200 word statement on the two world
rules; optional process evidence up to four images; and a contact block with lead entrant,
country, category and team members.

File names use the entry id issued at registration. Late or incomplete entries are
returned once with a 72 hour cure window; after that they are excluded.
## Judging
Screening removes ineligible or incomplete entries, then a panel scores the
shortlist. Each criterion is scored 0 to 5 and weighted.

| Criterion | Weight | What a 5 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Cultural authenticity | 25 | Reads as this village, not generic fantasy |
| Design originality | 25 | A new combination of silhouette, motif and material |
| Craft feasibility | 20 | Can be made with the stated process at the stated tolerance |
| Heritage respect | 20 | Cites shared technique, claims nothing exclusive |
| Presentation | 10 | Boards are legible and the statement is specific |

Weighted score is score times weight, divided by 5; a winner needs at least 3.5 and
no criterion below 2, with ties broken on heritage respect then craft feasibility. The
panel has three internal roles (design, craft, licensing) and two external designers.
Judges sign a conflict declaration and withdraw from entries they advised; scores are
recorded per criterion, not as one vote. Shortlisted entrants get one paragraph on
their strongest and weakest criterion; winners get a heritage note for production.
## IP and Licensing
Entrants retain copyright. By entering they grant the company a non-exclusive licence
to display, reproduce and archive the entry for judging, results and internal review,
for 12 months after the results date. Shortlisted entrants grant a 90 day option to
negotiate a derivative licence; the option is non-exclusive and does not transfer
ownership.

Winners sign a derivative licence built from `docs/licensing-templates.md`. It fixes
the category, territory, term, royalty and merchandise specification. No winner is
promised exclusivity over a heritage technique or a shared motif, and no entry may be
licensed exclusively while it references a traditional prototype. Production starts
only after the signed licence and the approved merchandise specification exist.

Prizes, set by the company before announcement: a production royalty under the signed
derivative licence; a studio visit and technical review; a cash honorarium per
category, published at launch; and credit on the product page and packaging insert.
## Heritage and Data
The competition reuses the bible's three world rules and its heritage position, and
every entrant pack states them in plain language. Before any shortlist is published
the reviewer checks that no entry claims ownership of a regional technique or a shared
motif; reproduces a ritual object, a sacred site or a protected pattern; copies an
existing licensed product line; fails to disclose AI assistance and the tool
category; or fails to name a craftsperson or process for the finishing step.

An entry that fails a heritage check is removed, not down-scored. Removal is recorded
with the reviewer, the rule and the evidence. The entrant may appeal once, in writing,
within seven days.

Data collected: name, email, country, category, team members, portfolio link and
submission files. No special category data is requested; age is confirmed by
declaration and entrants under 18 are not admitted.

Data controls: judging and administration only; access limited to the competition lead,
judging coordinator and heritage reviewer; company-controlled storage; deletion 12
months after results or on request, except the winner's licensing file; publicity uses
name and country only with consent; AI disclosure stays with the entry record.

Entrants may withdraw before the submission deadline and have their files deleted.
Withdrawal after judging starts removes the entry from ranking but not from the audit
record.
## Operations and Risk
Roles: competition lead (schedule, decisions, escalation, public statements);
judging coordinator (panel, scores, conflicts, tie breaks); entrant support (inbox,
questions, cure window, withdrawals); heritage reviewer (rule checks, removals,
heritage notes); licensing lead (options, winner licences, production handoff);
communications (announcement, results, social copy, press questions).

The company provisions a registration form and a monitored mailbox. This document
does not define endpoints, fields or integrations; those are set by the company at
launch and are outside the product contract. Weekly rhythm: a Monday status note with
registrations, submissions, open questions and risks; a Thursday check of the inbox
and the cure list; a Friday update to the decision log. Every decision that changes a
rule is written down and published to entrants within 24 hours.

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Heritage misuse in entries | medium | high | Entrant pack, pre-review, removal rule, appeal path |
| Low entry volume | medium | medium | Early outreach, two question sessions, one reminder |
| Judging bias or conflict | low | high | Conflict declarations, per-criterion scores, external judges |
| IP dispute after award | low | high | Rights clearance declaration, option agreement, counsel review |
| Schedule slip | medium | medium | Single announced date change, no rolling deadline |
| Prize expectation mismatch | medium | medium | Publish prize structure at launch, no verbal promises |
| Winner cannot be produced | medium | high | Craft feasibility weight, technical review before licence |
| Data incident | low | high | Named access, 12 month retention, deletion on request |
## Metrics, Review and Verification
Targets: registrations set at launch from the outreach list; at least 20 valid
submissions across at least 3 categories; at least 5 countries represented; a 100
percent heritage pass rate on the shortlist after pre-review; judging completed on
schedule, recorded yes or no; at least one winner licence signed within 90 days; and
at least one production-ready specification. Metrics are reported once, in the
post-event review, with raw counts and the decision log. A metric without a recorded
source is not reported.

Post-event review, within 30 days: participation, judging, heritage, licensing,
operations and cost; three changes for the next edition, each with an owner; and a
decision to run again, change theme or stop. The review is stored with the decision
log, and the next edition starts from this document and the review, not from memory.
