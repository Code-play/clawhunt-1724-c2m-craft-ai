# Licensing Templates and Merchandise Specifications
Operating reference for licence package IPL-0001. Source data lives in
`config/ip/licensing.json`; the generated pack is `out/licensing_pack.json`. The
package covers four derivative categories, five documents and four merchandise
specifications.

All parties are synthetic: Craft Village IP Office, Harbour Jewellery Works and the
Regional craft association. Nothing here is legal advice; counsel reviews the
signed set before commercial use.

## Template Clauses
Grant: licensor grants licensee a non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable
right to reproduce A-Mu (CHR-MU) and A-Huo (CHR-HUO) and the approved motifs solely
in the derivative categories in Schedule A, for the stated territory and term. No
right is granted to the heritage technique, a traditional prototype or third-party
material.

Territory: [CN, EU, US] for the package. A document may narrow it: MS-0002 is CN and
EU only, MS-0004 is CN and US only. Sales outside the document territory need a
signed addendum, not a verbal approval.

Term: 36 months from the effective date for the package. MS-0002 and MS-0004 run for
24 months. Stock manufactured inside the term may be sold for [90] days after
expiry only if the royalty for that stock was already reported.

Royalty: 800 basis points (8.00 percent) of the net wholesale invoice for the
licensed product, reported per quarter and paid within [30] days of the quarter
close. Where a merchandise specification states a different rate, that rate applies
to that product: MS-0002 600 bp, MS-0003 1000 bp, MS-0004 700 bp. Returns and
documented discounts reduce the royalty base; freight, tax and gift wrapping do not.

Quality: every product must meet the bill of materials, tolerances, finish, testing
and sampling plan in its merchandise specification. AQL values are fixed per spec and
may not be relaxed by the licensee. A failed inspection lot is reworked or rejected
at the licensee's cost.

Labelling: every unit carries an authenticity label and the licence number. The
label and the licence number are printed on the paper band or packaging, never on
the piece itself. Packaging must also carry the craftsperson name where the
specification requires it, plus market-language care text. A unit without a legible
label may not ship.

Heritage non-exclusive: licensor makes no exclusive claim over the regional
mortise-and-tenon joinery technique, the local kiln-fire glaze technique or the
peony roundel as a shared decorative motif. Those references are cited as shared
practice. Nothing in the agreement grants exclusivity over them, and no affiliate may
register them.

Termination: either party may terminate for material breach after 30 days written
notice and a failure to cure. Licensor may terminate immediately for a heritage
breach: an exclusive claim, a reproduced ritual object, or use outside the approved
categories. On termination the licensee stops manufacture, sells down approved stock
under the royalty clause, and returns or destroys unapproved artwork.
### Character Licence
Use this document to license the characters themselves, including silhouette,
palette, motifs, taboos, props and voice. Schedule A lists the four derivative
categories and the approved visual assets. A character licence without the
derivative schedule is incomplete.
### Derivative Licence
Use this document to license a specific derivative category and product family. It
carries the bill of materials reference, the merchandise spec id, the royalty
override and the territory. It does not grant character rights by itself.
### Merchandising
Use this document for retail packaging, point-of-sale material and gift sets. It
governs the label position, the licence number placement and the market language. It
currently covers the pendant and the cultural gift.
### Territorial Addendum
Use this document to add or narrow a market for an existing signed licence. It must
name the parent document id and repeat the royalty and term unchanged unless the
parties sign a separate commercial term sheet.
### Extended Markets
Draft template for markets beyond CN, EU and US. It is deliberately unsigned in this
package: it exists so a future expansion has a starting document, and it must not be
cited as coverage until both parties sign.

## Quality and Labelling Baseline
Package-wide material standard: recycled precious metal, FSC hardwood and OEKO-TEX
textiles; every material declares its sourcing class, `certified` or `licensed`.

Packaging rule: kraft or FSC card box with a paper band. The authenticity label and
the licence number are printed on the band, never on the piece.

Five inspection points apply to the package: authenticity label present and legible;
licence number matches the signed document; character motif matches the approved
visual asset; finish matches the specification; packaging band matches the product.
## INV-IP-01 and INV-IP-02
INV-IP-01, IP licensing scope, checks the usage block: every entry in
`traditional_prototype_refs` must keep `exclusive_claimed: false`; and if any
traditional prototype is referenced, `usage.exclusivity` must be false.

INV-IP-02, licence document coverage, checks the document set: document ids must be
unique; every signed document must carry both a licensor and a licensee signature;
every category in `usage.derivative_categories` must appear in at least one signed
document; and exclusivity cannot be granted while traditional prototypes are
referenced. Together they make two failures impossible: an exclusive claim over
shared heritage, and a licensed category with no signed paper behind it.

Negative samples cover an exclusive claim with heritage refs, a category covered only
by a draft, and a merchandise spec with no authenticity label.
## Pre-Publish Checklist
- The category you are shipping appears in a signed document, not a draft.
- The merchandise spec id is cited in the licence and matches the product; royalty,
  term and territory are copied from the spec.
- The bill of materials declares a sourcing class, and tolerances are positive.
- The AQL is the spec value; the label and licence number sit on the band.
- No exclusivity is claimed over a heritage technique or shared motif; the content
  review id is recorded.
